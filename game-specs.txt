BRIDGEBUILDER MR - GAME SPECS (as built on the `testing` branch)
================================================================

The design doc with the reasons behind each decision is docs/spec.md
(section 11 covers the playtest changes summarized here).

Every tuning value lives in Assets/BridgeBuilder.Core/Constants.*.cs and is tagged:
  HUMAN_OWNED - only a person changes it (Constants.Human.cs is never edited by agents)
  GUESS       - placeholder to tune in the headset
  DERIVED     - follows from physics or a unit conversion


1. WHAT IT IS
-------------
A mixed-reality bridge-building game for Meta Quest 2, for students new to physics
and civil engineering. Passthrough shows the real room. You mark two ends, draw a
bridge in the air, and send a car across. Members change color with load, and
anything that breaks says why in plain words.

  Engine:   Unity 6000.5.1f1, URP
  XR:       OpenXR + Meta OpenXR, AR Foundation passthrough, XRI MR template
  Target:   Meta Quest 2, Android min API 32 (set automatically by QuestBuildSettings)
  Physics:  BridgeBuilder.Core - plain C#, no Unity code, tested with xUnit


2. GAME FLOW
------------
1. Brief:   pick one of four challenges with the right stick, press A.
2. Place:   two trigger taps mark the start and end. Each becomes a 0.3 x 0.3 m pad.
            Taps must be at least 0.3 m apart horizontally. If the floor is at least
            0.15 m below the lower pad, a grid of floor dots (0.10 m apart) appears
            for piers.
3. Build:   draw lines and check the dead-load colors.
4. Testing: the car drives from the start pad to the other pad. The test runs on a
            copy, so going back to building restores the bridge as built.


3. CONTROLS
-----------
  Right trigger (hold)   Build: draw a line, release to place it
                         Brief: start / Place: mark an end
  Right grip             Build: delete the line under the tip
  A                      Build: start the test / Brief: start / Testing: back to building
  B                      Build: undo
  X                      Build: clear (press twice within 2 s); with nothing built, back to brief
                         Place: undo last tap / Testing: back to building
  Y                      Build: swap which pad the car starts on
  Right stick L/R        Brief: choose a challenge

  Keyboard over Link: Space = A, Backspace = B, Delete = grip, R = X, Y = Y, arrows = stick


4. BUILDING RULES
-----------------
Free drawing:
  - A stroke can start anywhere in space.
  - On release it's resampled, smoothed (moving average, 3 samples each side,
    1 cm apart), straightened where nearly straight, then split into equal
    segments with dots about 0.10 m apart.
  - Strokes shorter than 0.04 m are rejected.
  - A stroke joins any dot, pad top or floor dot it passes within 0.04 m of.
    The cursor turns blue when a join is under the tip.

Supports:
  - The whole top of each pad is an anchor. A line end touching a pad becomes a
    fixed support. Floor dots work the same way, for piers.

Automatic road - a deck forms between two drawn lines when:
  - both run from one pad to the other,
  - they're side by side, 0.08-0.30 m apart,
  - they're within 20 degrees of parallel,
  - the slope is at most 20 degrees.
  The deck is triangulated with hidden braces (shortest-diagonal zigzag) and has
  weight and stiffness like any member. The panel says "Road ready" when it's valid.

Deleting and limits:
  - Grip deletes the line under the tip (within 3 cm).
  - A line other lines hang from can't be deleted until those are gone; the
    blocking members flash red.
  - Each challenge has a material budget in room meters of line.
  - Node cap: 150 (HUMAN_OWNED) + 60 extra for pad and floor supports.


5. CHALLENGES
-------------
  First crossing   "Get a car across. Hint: triangles are strong."
                   1.5 t car, no wind, grip 0.9, 14 m material
  Heavy truck      "A 12 tonne truck. Spread its weight."
                   12 t, no wind, grip 0.9, 18 m material
  Crosswind        "A 15 m/s wind pushes from the side."
                   1.5 t, 15 m/s wind, grip 0.9, 16 m material
  Icy and windy    "Slippery tires and a 10 m/s wind."
                   1.5 t, 10 m/s wind, grip 0.3, 16 m material

  PASS: the car reaches the far pad (within 0.25 m, HUMAN_OWNED) before the 30 s
        timeout without falling (airborne 0.5 s counts as falling).
  3 STARS: no members broken, peak load <= 80%, >= 25% of material left
           (GUESS until HG-stars decides). Otherwise no stars.


6. SCALE AND MATERIAL
---------------------
Scale: 1 m in the room = 20 m of bridge (GUESS). Physics runs in bridge-world SI units.

Material - steel placeholders (HUMAN_OWNED, pending HG-material):
  Young's modulus E   2.0e11 Pa
  Shear modulus G     7.69e10 Pa
  Density             7850 kg/m^3
  Yield strength      250e6 Pa (GUESS)

One cross-section for every member (GUESS, calibrated against fixtures 1 and 5):
  Area A                       4.0e-3 m^2
  Second moment I (both axes)  1.6e-5 m^4
  Torsion constant J           1.0e-6 m^4 (HUMAN_OWNED)
  Outer fiber distance c       0.1 m

Calibration results:
  Fixture 1, bare cantilever   1.97              fails (required >= 1.0)
  Fixture 5, truss             0.027             holds (required < 1.0)
  Bare deck in game            0.99 under car    holds itself, fails under the car
  Truss bridge in game         0.35              passes


7. PHYSICS MODEL
----------------
Structure:
  - Each member is a 3D Euler-Bernoulli frame element (12 DOF, 6 per node).
    Joints are rigid.
  - Stiffness matrix uses reverse Cuthill-McKee ordering and a banded Cholesky solver.

Member capacity - utilization is the worst of:
  Tension       axial force / (A * Fy)
  Compression   axial force / (A * Fy)
  Buckling      axial force / Euler critical load, K = 0.5 (HUMAN_OWNED),
                member length measured between junctions
  Bending       M * c / I, divided by Fy
  A member breaks at 1.0. Failure is progressive: at most 3 members removed per
  step, so collapses unfold over several frames. Loose pieces fall as debris (XPBD).

Dynamics:
  Integration   Newmark average acceleration (beta 0.25, gamma 0.5, DERIVED) at 60 Hz
  Damping       Rayleigh, ratio 0.02 at 1 Hz and 20 Hz (GUESS)
  Test start    bridge settles under its own weight (static solve), then the car starts
  Gravity       9.80665 m/s^2

Car (all GUESS, pending HG-car-and-wind):
  Four wheels with suspension, coupled to the bridge (bridge state lagged one step).
  Springs sized for 0.12 m of sag at rest.
  Wheelbase 2.7 m, track 1.6 m, length 4.5 m, spring rest length 0.6 m,
  damping 0.5 of critical, cruise speed 8 m/s.
  Wind: side area 6 m^2, front 2.5 m^2, drag 1.0; members drag 1.2 at 0.2 m diameter.
  Air density 1.225 kg/m^3 (DERIVED).

Threading:
  - Physics runs on a worker thread with triple-buffered snapshots, so the
    headset never waits on the solver.
  - The dead-load preview runs 0.3 s after the last edit and is canceled if
    drawing starts again.


8. FEEDBACK
-----------
While building: colors show how hard the bridge works to hold itself up.
Purple = not attached to anything, will fall.

During a test (GUESS thresholds):
  Color          green < 50%, yellow 50-80%, red > 80%
  Pulse          at 90% and above
  Stripes        denser with load, 1-8
  Buckling bow   up to 5 cm
  Creak sound    pitch 80 -> 400 Hz as the worst member nears failure
  Break marker   pulsing red dot where a member broke

Failure messages shown to students:
  Tension      "Snapped: it was pulled harder than the steel can stretch. Share the pull with more members."
  Compression  "Crushed: it was squeezed harder than the steel can take. Add members to share the push."
  Buckling     "Buckled: long, thin members bow sideways when squeezed. Make them shorter with triangles."
  Bending      "Bent: a long beam sagged too far. Support it from below or above with triangles."

Solver stats: the grey line at the bottom of the panel shows member count, ms per
step, test start time and preview time. Use it to benchmark the node cap on the headset.
